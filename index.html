About

I like to understand things by building them from first principles. That has meant writing transformers by hand to reproduce results like grokking, and building a C++ tensor library as the base for a transformer inference engine. My physics background shapes how I think about optimization: loss landscapes, training dynamics, and why models generalize when they do.

Right now I'm researching LLM-generated scheduling policies in distributed systems at UW–Madison. This past summer I built evaluation infrastructure for voice AI agents at T-Mobile. I'm also part of the Wisconsin AI Safety Initiative and completed BlueDot's Technical AI Safety course.
